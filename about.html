WHO WE ARE

Expert Support for Your Research Journey

ThesisToBook is a thesis-to-book conversion and publishing support platform dedicated to helping authors present their knowledge to a wider audience. We understand the effort, dedication and expertise that go into academic research.

Our approach focuses on preserving the originality of your work while adapting its structure, presentation and readability for a book format.

Whether you're completing a PhD thesis, a postgraduate dissertation or an independent research project, we aim to provide support tailored to your manuscript and goals.
