PRICING QUESTIONS

Before You Choose

Understand what affects your quote and package selection.

Are the prices fixed?

The displayed amounts are proposed starting prices. Your final quotation should confirm the manuscript scope, deliverables, applicable taxes and any additional costs.

How do I choose the right package?

Consider the editing, restructuring, design and publishing guidance you need. If you're unsure, submit an enquiry so your requirements can be assessed before a package is agreed.

Can I request services outside a package?

Yes, you can enquire about a custom scope. Availability, pricing and deliverables need to be confirmed for your project.

Does publishing assistance guarantee publication?

No. Assistance with preparing a manuscript or exploring publishing routes does not guarantee publisher acceptance, distribution or sales.

Are printing and distribution included?

Do not assume these costs are included in a package. Confirm the specific production, printing, distribution and platform fees in your final quotation.
